INTRODUCTION

=== PAGE 3 ===
3 ---
Polaris International Industries LLC is an integrated industrial set-up in Dubai, with the express
mission of providing high-quality manufacturing services in the furniture and interior fit-out space.
Primarily targeting clients within the geographical area of the Gulf Co-operation Council we also
cater to discerning clients around the globe.
We have a well-equipped facility that is functionally laid out over a 45,000 square-foot area so as to
make it one of the most efﬁcient, advanced and sophisticated manufacturing environment which is
located at the Dubai Investment Park.
Our state-of-the-art machinery along with our superior workmanship ensures a superlative product
and the ﬁnest range of Fitted joinery, metal and glasswork delivered to our valued clients.
We believe we are diametrically different from space predominantly because We don’t do different
things, We do them differently our way.
We have learnt over the years of our professional happy customer; we also understand that every
happy customer we have is our best brand ambassador and that eventually, commercial success will
follow as a natural consequence of our whole-hearted endeavors.
The manufacturing facilities are ﬁtted out with multi-purpose machinery that caters to the custom
manufacture of premium furniture and ﬁt-out for hotels, apartments, ofﬁces, showrooms, and retail
shop-ﬁtting. Though Polaris prides itself on its manufacturing abilities, we also offer design support
services as an in-house offering.

QUALITY 
POLICY

=== PAGE 15 ===
15 ---
We are committed to a continual process of on-going improvement. 
that is why our company’s quality policy conforms to the 
management system standard iso 9001-2008 as well as to all the 
national and international applicable standards, supplying all the 
resources needed to implement the following
Policy implemented in all areas of Operations by: 
•
Ensuring a clear understanding of our client requirements 
•
Providing the resources to achieve the quality objectives 
•
Creating management mechanism aimed at optimum utilization 
of creativity, abilities, knowledge, expertise and skills of all level 
employees 
•
Guarantee the delivery of products in accordance with the 
relevant requirements 
•
Reduction in non-conformities as a result of good planning and 
manufacturing practices, procedures are planned around the 
prevention of defects and where nonconformities occurs, it is our 
objective to provide a fast efficient response to correct the non-
conformity and prevent recurrence. 
Ours priority is to achieve the highest standards of performance 
throughout its business operations and in the dealings with 
customers and suppliers. This quality policy supports these 
objectives and is understood, implemented and maintained at all 
levels within the company

HSE POLICY

=== PAGE 17 ===
17 ---
POLARIS will ensure that all operations are carried out while paying 
due regard to moral and legislative requirements imposed upon them. 
This will enable the contract to be undertaken with the provision of 
appropriate safeguards to prevent or mitigate potential damage to the 
environment.
POLARIS will pursue compliance and continual improvement in its 
environmental performance from year to year, this shall happen 
through:
Implementation and maintenance of an effective Environmental 
Management System (EMS).
• Accepting management responsibilities to ensure full compliance by 
all employees to Vedas International, national and applicable 
worldwide environmental standards, policies and procedures 
wherever relevant.
• The regular assessment and review of the environmental aspects 
and impacts of our activities in order to set realistic objectives and 
targets which meet the needs of the business, the environment, our 
employees and our neighborhood. 
• Establishing and auditing Environmental Safety procedures and 
responsibilities to ensure that our workplace and work on 
construction sites is environmentally acceptable.
• Implementing appropriate preventive measures by establishing and 
enforcing environmentally sound method of work.
• Ensure design, engineering and process improvements and 
practices, reduce negative environmental impacts, waste, energy, 
raw material consumption and prevent pollution.
• Ensure resources are available to meet these objectives.
POLARIS will ensure that all operations are carried whilst paying due 
regard to moral, ethical and legislative requirements imposed upon them. 
This will enable the contract to be undertaken with the provision of 
safeguards to prevent any members of the public, employees or sub-
contractors being exposed to risks to their Health and Safety.
As it is the aim of POLARIS to achieve a safe working environment, which 
is free from accidents and ill-health, we will pursue continuing 
improvements from year to year.
This aim will be achieved within the Company's organization and 
arrangements for the provision of Health, Safety and Welfare, by:
• Identifying hazards and assessing the risks.
• Implementing appropriate preventative and protective measures.
• Establishing and enforcing safe methods of work.
• Providing and maintaining safe plant and work equipment.
• Recruiting and appointing personnel who have the skills, abilities and 
competence commensurate with their role and level of responsibility.
• Ensuring that tasks given to employees are within their skills, knowledge 
and ability to perform.
• Ensuring that technical competence is maintained through the provision 
of refresher training as appropriate.
• Promoting awareness of health, safety and environment and of good 
practice through the effective communication of relevant information.
• Establishing and auditing Health and Safety procedures and 
responsibilities to ensure that our workplace and work on construction 
sites is safely and healthy acceptable.
• To maintain clear procedures for action to be taken in the event of an 
emergency.
• Ensuring resources are available to meet these objectives.
